01 My Bio

Before I had a title,I had a belief:life writes the first draft.

Great advertising starts in the street, in everyday conversations, habits, and contradictions.I look for the overlooked detail and make it the punchline, the moment that makes people relate.

I built my foundation as a copywriter in Egypt, then took that experience across MENA. Over 13+ years, I’ve developed integrated 360 campaigns, digital marketing strategies, films, and branded content, and led multidisciplinary teams of more than 30 creatives. Today, I’m Creative Director at SLANG Advertising, bringing that regional experience to the Iraqi market and leading teams in Iraq and Egypt.

02 How I think

Observe. Connect. One line. Express. Lead.

  1. 01 / Observe

    Notice what people actually do: habits, phrases, rituals a market already recognises.

  2. 02 / Connect

    Link people, an honest brand offer, and the culture they share.

  3. 03 / The idea

    Copywriter first: say it in one line people want to repeat.

  4. 04 / Express

    Let the line travel: scripts, films, series, social and 360 campaigns.

  5. 05 / Lead

    Build teams that make it happen: 30+ creatives across MENA, now Iraq and Egypt.
